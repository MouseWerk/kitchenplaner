Kitchenplaner 0.3.0

Connection points and the installation plan: every unit knows where its electric, water, drain, gas and air connections sit, the plan set prints a sheet per trade with its connection table, and the DXF hands each point to CAD with its values.

## Improved

- Every unit has connection points per trade: electric, cold, hot and softened water, drain, floor drain, gas, exhaust, supply air, steam and remote refrigeration. Each point has its place on the unit and its height above the finished floor („ü. FFB“), with typical values from the catalog: current, fuse and plug for electric (230 V 1N~ or 400 V 3N~), nominal size for pipes and ducts, greasy drains marked.
- The inspector lists the points under „Anschlusspunkte“. Open a point to set its distance from the left and back edge, its height, current, fuse, plug, nominal size, „fetthaltig“ and a remark. Typical values show in grey, your own values in bold; „Zurücksetzen“ returns a point to the catalog.
- „Anschlusswerte“ adds softened water and, under „Weitere Anschlüsse“, supply air, steam and remote refrigeration. The heat a unit gives off into the kitchen is estimated as a typical value and can be overwritten.
- „Darstellung“ has a trade filter: „Alle Anschlüsse“, „Nur Elektro“, „Nur Sanitär“, „Nur Gas“, „Nur Lüftung“, „Nur Kälte und Dampf“. The floor plan then shows the equipment quietly and the points with their number and height on top; the 3D view shows them as small stubs.
- The PDF plan set adds an installation plan per trade with symbols, dimensions from the walls and a legend, each followed by its connection table. The electric table sums the kW, plain and with the simultaneity factor.
- New exports: „Installationsplan als SVG / DXF / PNG…“ and „Anschlussliste als CSV…“, following the trade filter.
- In the DXF every connection point is a block on its trade layer (Elektro, Gas, Wasser, Abwasser, Lüftung, Dampf, Kälte) with its number, height and values as attributes.
- The plan check knows the connections: a gas unit without a hood, a missing gas emergency shut-off, a unit standing free in the room without a floor or ceiling connection, a floor drain point without a gully or channel, a hood whose airflow is short of the heat below it, a fuse below the current, and a point outside the room or its unit. Heat and hood airflow are typical values, not a proof; a full gas line under a small wall hood is reported.
- The equipment list shows softened water, supply air and the heat release.
- The example kitchen gets two floor boxes under its islands and now has 67 positions.

## Fixed

- Project files are format version 4. Kitchenplaner 0.3 opens every earlier file unchanged; Kitchenplaner 0.2 refuses a 0.3 file instead of dropping its connection points.

## Checklist for Windows (manual)

- Let 0.2.3 update itself with „Jetzt aktualisieren“: Kitchenplaner restarts as 0.3.0.
- Open a project saved with 0.2.3: every unit and value is still there; save it and open it again.
- Open `Beispielkueche-Restaurant.kplan` from the release: 67 positions, no issues.
- Select the combi oven: „Anschlusspunkte“ lists E, KW, EW and AW. Open E, set „Höhe ü. FFB“ to 650: the value shows in bold; Strg+Z takes it back.
- Choose Darstellung → „Nur Elektro“: the plan shows the electric points with their numbers and heights; the 3D view shows the stubs. Switch back to „Anschlüsse aus“.
- Export „Plansatz als PDF…“ on A3: there is an installation plan for each trade with points, each followed by its connection table; the electric table ends with the kW sums.
- Export „Installationsplan als DXF…“ and open it in a CAD program: clicking a point selects one block whose attributes show its number, height and values; hiding the layer ANSCHLUSS_ELEKTRO hides every electric point with its labels.
- Export „Anschlussliste als CSV…“ and open it in Excel: one row per point with the wall distances and the height.
- Switch a gas range from gas to electric: the gas point disappears and the electric point appears.
