Kitchenplaner 0.2.3

Every wall gets its own elevation, the DXF export arrives in CAD as real blocks with their values, the plan check knows the clearances a kitchen needs, and large kitchens turn smoothly in 3D.

## Improved

- Every wall has its own elevation, slanted walls included. Each elevation has one chooser: the four directions as before, and below them every wall of the room („Wand 3 (Nordost, 1.414 mm)“).
- „Alle Ansichten als SVG…“ and the plan set add a sheet for each slanted wall. In the plan set, „Ansichten einzelner Wände“ chooses none, slanted walls or every wall.
- The DXF export is AutoCAD 2000 format with units in millimetres. Each unit is a block you can select and move in CAD as one piece, with its position number, name, size, manufacturer and connection values as attributes. Layers follow the trades: walls, openings, equipment, labels, dimensions, and electric, gas, water, drain and ventilation.
- The plan check knows the clearances of a commercial kitchen:
  - 1 × 1 m movement area in front of every workplace, and 1.2 m between workplaces facing each other
  - free space in front of doors, oven doors, tilting units, roll-in units and the hood dishwasher
  - 0.8 m between shelving rows
  - hoods overhanging the units below by 200 mm on every open side, and 400 mm in front of steaming ovens
  - a hand wash basin within reach of every production area

  Each finding gives the measured and the required value; clicking it selects the units involved.
- Large kitchens turn smoothly in 3D: with 500 units a frame takes a tenth of the time it did.
- The catalog draws only the previews you see, without stalling the window, and remembers them for the next start.
- A cut-off or damaged project file is reported as damaged, not as a foreign file.

## Fixed

- The example kitchen keeps its own clearances: the vegetable sink has room behind it, and the mop sink stands between the dish shelves.

## Checklist for Windows (manual)

- Let 0.2.2 update itself with „Jetzt aktualisieren“: Kitchenplaner restarts as 0.2.3.
- Open `Beispielkueche-Restaurant.kplan` from the release: the status bar shows no issues.
- Move a table directly in front of the range: the plan check reports the movement area with the measured and the required value; clicking the finding selects both units.
- Draw a room with a 45° corner (Raum, wall tool). In an elevation pane, choose the slanted wall from the chooser: the wall shows straight on with its length. Export „Alle Ansichten als SVG…“: there is a sheet for the slanted wall.
- Export the plan as DXF and open it in a CAD program (DWG TrueView, BricsCAD, LibreCAD or QCAD): it opens without a repair message, in millimetres, and clicking a unit selects it as one block whose attributes show Pos., name and connection values.
- Make the window 1000 px wide with the overview: every elevation pane shows its chooser and its cut depth.
- Select all units of the example kitchen in the overview and turn the 3D view: it follows smoothly. Close and start Kitchenplaner again: the catalog previews are there at once.
