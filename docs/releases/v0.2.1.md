Kitchenplaner 0.2.1

A catalog more than twice as large: 359 units instead of 165, from the 700 cooking line to the bar, the bakery and the building services, and every unit reworked to look like the real thing.

## Improved

- 194 new units, 359 in all, each with its plan symbol, the four elevations, a 3D model, a catalog preview and typical connection values:
  - Cooking (41 new, 77 in all): the 700 line (range with oven, fryer, griddle, pasta cooker, bain-marie, lava stone grill, bratt pan, tilting pan, base cabinet, hob cover), fixed bratt pans, a three-burner wok range, teppanyaki, a radiant charbroiler, a heated overshelf with infrared lamps and a plain overshelf, a mixing kettle and a multifunction cooking unit, cook and hold ovens and mobile holding cabinets, a smoking cabinet, conveyor, stone dome and rotating deck pizza ovens, rotisserie, doner grill and tandoor, and countertop units: steamer, microwaves, lift-up salamander, griddles, induction wok, crêpe maker, waffle iron, contact grill, conveyor toaster, egg boiler, rice cooker and sous-vide bath.
  - Cooling (26 new, 54 in all): roll-in refrigerators and freezers, a roll-in blast chiller for two trolleys and a countertop blast chiller, pass-through, fish and dry-aging cabinets, a retarder-proofer and a bakery freezer for 600 × 400 trays, a wine climate cabinet, a beverage cooler with sliding doors, a back-bar cooler, an ice cream display and freezer, a chest freezer, a countertop display and a refrigerated pass, heavy chef bases and 600 deep drawer counters, a drop-in cold well, an ice bin, an ice and water dispenser, a drinking water cooler, a beverage cold room with glass doors, a freezer room door and a condensing unit.
  - Washing and hygiene (29 new, 53 in all): sinks with the bowl in the middle and corner sinks, dish tables with the machine on the other side, a double-rack hood dishwasher, a front loader on a base cabinet, a glass washer with drain pump, granule pot washer, tray washer, cutlery polisher, ultrasonic tank, vegetable washer, boot washer and food waste disposer, a tray return belt and trolley, a rack dispenser and cutlery soaking trolley, a pedestal hand wash basin, sanitiser dispensers, an eye wash station, cleaning and dosing stations, a cleaning trolley and a water softener.
  - Tables and preparation (29 new, 56 in all): granite, pastry, cutting, GN and hand wash tables, a butcher block, mobile GN tables, utility and machine trolleys, a board rack; spiral mixers, a dough divider and rounder, a bread slicer and a proofer, a bowl cutter, bone saw, tenderiser and burger press, a vacuum tumbler, stick and cooking blenders, a juicer, ice crusher, tempering and pasta machines, a sauce dispenser, a label scale and printer.
  - Serving (27 new, 45 in all): hot, refrigerated and neutral buffet islands, chafing dishes, soup kettles, carving lamps, heated displays, holding drawers, a tray stand, cutlery and juice dispensers; espresso machine, coffee grinder, filter coffee, hot water dispenser, milk cooler, cup warmer and knock box module; a bar counter, draught column, flow cooler, glass froster, ice well with speed rail, cocktail station and back bar; soft-serve and frozen yoghurt machines.
  - Storage (17 new, 32 in all): mobile shelving, cold room, GN, bread, tray and wine racks, wall plate and pot racks, a plate stacking trolley, cutlery, cleaning agent and linen cabinets, staff lockers, euro pallet, stacking crates, a crate dolly and a sack truck.
  - Ventilation (9 new, 15 in all): wall canopies with UV technology and with supply air, a ventilated ceiling with supply air, a vapour hood for the dishwasher, a downdraft module, a swirl diffuser, a displacement outlet, a fire suppression nozzle line and an exhaust fan box.
  - Building elements (16 new, 27 in all): round and bucket floor gullies, an under-sink grease trap, a lifting station, a distribution board, a gas emergency shut-off, fire extinguisher and fire blanket, an emergency exit sign, wall and corner guards, a clad column, a ceiling service column, a floor box, a radiator and a water heater.
- Every unit of the earlier catalog looks more like the real thing: turned knobs, feet and bowls, pressed housings with their control panels and displays, doors with gaskets and handles on stand-offs, baskets and grates made of rods, food in the wells and bottles in the coolers. Safety parts show their signal colours: red extinguishers, blankets and 400 V sockets, green exit signs, yellow gas levers, red and blue water marks.
- The catalog is divided further: „Kochen“, „Kühlen“, „Spülen & Hygiene“ and „Ausgabe“ show sub-headings such as „Herdlinie 900“, „Herdlinie 700“, „Tischgeräte“, „Kühlzellen“, „Spülmaschinen und Waschgeräte“ and „Bar“, each with its count. The search also finds units by these names.

## Fixed

- The charbroiler and the lava stone grill no longer look alike from above: the charbroiler's grates run across over bright radiants.
- The teppanyaki plate shows its front rail, the proofer its lit chamber with the trays behind the glass, and roll-in cabinets their bump rails, so they no longer pass for an upright refrigerator.
- Wide units stay quick in 3D: the widest fryers, pasta cookers, wine cabinets, passes and back bars show fewer but evenly spaced basket wires, bottles and plates.

## Checklist for Windows (manual)

- Install `Kitchenplaner_0.2.1_x64-setup.exe` over 0.2.0 (or let 0.2.0 update itself with „Jetzt aktualisieren“): Kitchenplaner starts as 0.2.1.
- Open the catalog: „Kochen“ shows 77 units under sub-headings („Herdlinie 900“, „Herdlinie 700“, …); scroll through the whole catalog: every row gets its preview within a few seconds and scrolling stays smooth. Search for „herdlinie 700“, „bar“ and „pizza“: the matching units appear under their sub-headings.
- Hover a few new units (teppanyaki, espresso machine, fire extinguisher, roll-in refrigerator): the preview shows the model with its connection values.
- Open `Beispielkueche-Restaurant.kplan` from the release and switch to 3D: the kitchen turns smoothly; add a fryer, a wine cabinet and a back bar at their widest width: 3D stays smooth.
- Place a gas charbroiler and a gas lava stone grill side by side: they look different from above in 3D and in the floor plan.
- Open a project saved with 0.2.0: every unit is still there with its position numbers and values.
